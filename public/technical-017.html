Value and measurement

Storage: content identity and occurrence identity stay separate; a queryable projection helps locate a failure. Storage savings NOT_COMPUTED.
Inference: bounded prompts and deterministic checks reduce what needs model judgment by design. Cost savings NOT_TESTED.
Troubleshooting: recorded fault, scan, review and recovery are inspectable. Decision speedup NOT_TESTED.
Cascades: failure never supplies authorization. This is demonstrated in the fixture, not established for arbitrary applications.
